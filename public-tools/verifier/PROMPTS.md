Open the extracted companion folder in Codex or Claude Code. Its project skills are included in both agents' discovery directories.

**Pair the browser.** “Start the local verifier watching `/path/to/paired-runs`. I will choose that same folder in the browser. Leave it running while I play, and show me the first completed receipt.”

If direct reduction exceeds the resources, retain the diagnostic, identify the expensive parser or tick interface and propose a decomposition with shared full-state boundaries. Mark it verified only after an accepted fresh import.”
