What does it mean to prove a game level?

You watch a player cross the threshold, press Use and reach the exit. What would let someone else check that the same controls really win from the ordinary starting state? The answer depends on exactly which game program interprets the controls. This chapter follows the path from a WAD file through a Lean function to a theorem about every prefix of a particular run. Three programs, three questions

DOOM. id Software’s game advances a simulation in discrete steps called tics. The map data, player input and current world determine what happens next. A WAD is a container of named byte ranges called lumps. Map lumps encode such things as vertices, linedefs (oriented edges), sectors (regions with floor and ceiling heights) and things (placed entities). Why the middle of a passage is hard

A moving player changes several data structures

Consider the first movement attempt on the recorded Computer Station route. The collision query encounters a clip. Ammunition rises from 50 to 60 and the clip’s slot is removed. The player has to leave the old spatial lists and enter the lists for sector 60 and blockmap cell 846. The blockmap indexes objects by a spatial grid so that collision queries can limit the area they search. A correct move must preserve the structures on which the next query relies. The theorem returns the complete updated collision scene and an empty list of special lines crossed. The rest of this tick, including subsequent updates, is a separate obligation. Checked movement declaration and audit · Original movement code

Two identical pictures can have different futures

A screenshot is a projection π(s)\pi(s) of the full state. Position, sprite and health may match while a random-generator index, a door timer or a delayed projectile differs. Therefore π(s)=π(t)\pi(s)=\pi(t) does not in general give Te(s,k)=Te(t,k)T_e(s,k)=T_e(t,k). The Nuclear Plant witness records a total of 1,017 inputs. Its suffix proof after input 19 accounts for 998 more ticks and 65 ordered actor slots per tick. That is 64,870 slot updates before counting the movement, weapon, projectile and world stages. Changing the state used at a chunk boundary can invalidate the remaining certificate. Nuclear Plant record · Original random-number generator · Behavior-preserving simulations in CompCert

A runnable function may resist reduction in a proof

Lean elaborates a command into a term. A small kernel checks that the term has the claimed type. A tactic such as decide helps produce a term. A compiled program can execute operations that lack the equations one wants for reduction inside the logic. The upstream weapon controller includes a partial recursive routine. With default weapon state, progressing even without firing can enter that routine. Structural recursion on fuel gives the revised controller a defined result in every case. Opaque conversions and trigonometric calls in enemy code motivated the arithmetic revision. The equation Te(s,k)=tT_e(s,k)=t names the edition, so a successful check cannot silently transfer to another version. Axioms and computation · Kernel validation · Recursive definitions

What exactly does the endpoint establish?

On Phobos Anomaly the ordinary starting state offers 333 possible linedef targets to the Use dispatcher. For each choice, the resulting exit flag is false. This rules out an immediate dispatcher win from that state. A theorem that every future keyboard sequence fails would also have to cover all later reachable states. Conversely, knowing that a switch works from a chosen point gives no route from spawn to that point. These examples illustrate how the order and domain of quantifiers determine a theorem’s strength. How to read a long proof

Let Re(s,K)R_e(s,K) be the state obtained by consuming a list of inputs in order. If uu ends at tt and vv starts at the very same tt, the endpoints compose.

Re(s,u++v)=Re(Re(s,u),v).R_e(s,u{+\!+}v)=R_e(R_e(s,u),v).

A segment in the research proof also records safety over its prefixes. A prefix of u++vu{+\!+}v either lies entirely within uu or consists of all of uu followed by a prefix of vv. This observation explains the two parts of the safety argument when joining segments. The notebook proves the endpoint equation for every pair of airlock input lists by induction. A three-input equality checked by computation and a composition lemma proved for arbitrary lists belong to the same proof language. Worked append lemma · Induction and recursion

Goal. Construct a safe first-exit witness for a named map and edition.

  1. Identify the start. Establish the parse and ordinary-spawn equalities on the quoted data. Record how a reader can identify the bytes.

  2. Prove each interval. Expose the physics, interaction and actor updates needed for its starting state. Carry the full endpoint and per-prefix invariants forward.

  3. Join the intervals. Apply the composition rule at exactly matching endpoints. Check that the concatenation of lists equals the actual submitted tape.

  4. Discharge the exit. Check the final step, all proper-prefix exit flags, positive health and absence of errors. Use the witness to introduce the existential quantifier.

This organization follows Leslie Lamport’s practice of giving each proof step its own target and justification. A reader can enter the proof at the movement step, append lemma or final result. Lamport on structured proofs

A reading path

Learn enough Lean to read the certificates

  • Lean installation and editor. Install the toolchain manager and try the interactive infoview. Open a theorem and place the cursor inside its proof to see the current goal. Official installation guide

  • Jeremy Avigad, Leonardo de Moura, Soonho Kong, Sebastian Ullrich and contributors, Theorem Proving in Lean 4. Begin with propositions, proofs and equality. Then read the induction chapter alongside runAppend in the lab. Textbook · Induction and recursion

  • David Thrane Christiansen, Functional Programming in Lean. Pattern matching, algebraic data types and the Option/error-handling examples explain the shape of a total transition function and a parser that can fail. Book and exercises

  • The mathlib community, Mathematics in Lean. Work through existential witnesses and the uses of rw and apply. The quantifiers in a shortest-route theorem then become easier to read. Book · Logic exercises

  • Lean community learning resources. Choose a course or a browser exercise at a suitable difficulty. Community learning guide · Lean’s learning portal

Understand the trusted boundary

  • Leonardo de Moura and Sebastian Ullrich, The Lean 4 Theorem Prover and Programming Language. The systems paper explains how elaboration, tactics and compilation coexist with kernel checking. Authors’ paper

  • Lean Language Reference, Validating a Lean Proof. Read the discussion of verification and trusted components before interpreting an axiom report or an imported compiled module. Validation reference

  • Lean Language Reference, Recursive Definitions. Use the sections on structural recursion and partial definitions to explain why compiled evaluation and definitional reduction can expose different information. Recursive definitions

  • Lean Language Reference, Fixed-Precision Integers and Floating-Point Numbers. Learn why bit widths, overflow, conversion and rounding must be specified at arithmetic boundaries. Fixed-precision integers · Floating-point numbers

  • Lean textbook, Axioms and Computation. Use #print axioms on an actual declaration. Distinguish the calculus’s standard principles from added assumptions and placeholders. Axioms and computation

  • NIST, Secure Hash Standard. A digest associates an artifact with particular bytes. The logical content of those bytes is established through parsing and checking. FIPS 180-4

Go further into formal methods

  • Benjamin Pierce and collaborators, Software Foundations. The small-step semantics chapter models execution by individual transitions. The Hoare logic chapter connects programs, preconditions and postconditions. The examples use Rocq/Coq. Small-step semantics · Hoare logic

  • Adam Chlipala, Formal Reasoning About Programs. Explore invariants and compositional reasoning across transition systems. Compare the book’s invariants with per-prefix survival in a game run. Author’s book site

  • Xavier Leroy and the CompCert team, The CompCert C Verified Compiler. Its semantic-preservation statement gives a worked example of how to relate two different execution models and choose observable behaviors. Manual introduction · Formal preservation results

  • George Necula, Compiling with Proofs. A source of the producer/checker idea. A recipient can verify a supplied certificate against a stated property independently of how it was found. Dissertation record and abstract

  • Leslie Lamport, How to Write a 21st Century Proof. Study the hierarchical presentation and rewrite one of the lab arguments as a short tree of obligations before expanding the tactics. Author’s paper
